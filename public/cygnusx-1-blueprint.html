01 Project Overview

CygnusX-1 automates SBA Quote-to-Cash — CIP intake through spreading, collateral, underwriting, documentation, E-Tran, and closure — with a supervisor-coordinated swarm. A human underwriter of record approves every credit decision. The swarm compresses preparation, not the decision.

Scope in

7(a), Express, and 504 origination. Customer login, Business capture, consumer and commercial credit, OFAC, spreading, collateral, credit memo, E-Tran, dual-control ledger post.

Scope out (phase 1)

Servicing after seal, fully autonomous approval, syndications, and any product outside SBA 7(a)/504.

Autonomy

Launches at L2 (agent prepares, human approves). L3 only for named low-risk sub-tasks after MRM sign-off.

Accountability — the swarm is never the credit decision-maker. Every output is a recommendation with evidence. The underwriter’s approval is the action of record.

02 Data Model (Schema)

Account is the customer: a Person Account for an individual, a Business Account for the company. Deal is the file, and the red line is its master-detail to that Account. LOS Product is the loan — a Deal can hold more than one. Related parties, collateral, metrics, the credit decision, and requirements all hang off the loan.

How to read it — required fields have a red tick on the left. Blue lines are lookups. The red line is Deal to Account. A guarantor, co-borrower, or authorized signor is a Related Party on that LOS Product, pointing at an Account.

Orchestration rules

  • Supervisor dispatches only workers allowed for Process.currentStage. A documentation worker cannot call E-Tran.
  • Each worker has a service identity and a tool allowlist. Tokens are short-lived and tagged with processId.
  • Workers write canonical tables — they do not keep private shadow copies of Customer, Business, or Loan.
  • Evaluator rejects ungrounded claims (no artefactId) and low-confidence extraction. It never fills a gate.
  • Pre-application qualifies the customer: any one passing check (consumer credit, business credit, or fraud history) advances the file. The swarm cannot infer a pass.
  • ScorecardResult is advisory. Decision is a human row (Diana Okonkwo). guarantyNumber is written only from E-Tran.
  • Every message, tool call, and confidence score appends AuditEvent under processId. That spine is the replay log.
Fail-closed — consumer credit, commercial credit, and OFAC must pass to leave Pre-application. The swarm cannot infer a pass, invent a guarantyNumber, or post the ledger. Dual-control posting is human.

05 Unstructured Data Cleansing & RAG

Inputs are PDFs, tax returns, appraisals, and scans. Retrieval quality is bounded by cleansing before index.

  • Classify — tax return, statement, appraisal, insurance. Unreadable files quarantine.
  • OCR — layout-aware tables; per-field confidence. Low confidence is flagged, not accepted.
  • Normalize — common chart of accounts; reconcile units and conflicting figures.
  • Chunk — by logical section, not character count. Metadata: processId, partyId, date, source.
  • Index — entitlement tags from the source system. Re-index on amendment; versions retained.
Quality gate — nothing enters the RAG index below the extraction confidence threshold. Garbage grounding produces confidently wrong credit output.

10 Cyber Incident Response — Agent Breach

Treat a compromised worker as a privileged service account, not a chatbot.

  • Kill switch: disable supervisor dispatch; freeze outbound tool calls.
  • Rotate the worker’s identity and revoke in-flight tokens.
  • Replay the audit spine for that processId; quarantine embeddings written after the event.
  • Notify per bank CSIRT. Credit files touched are in-scope for the incident.

11 Hallucination or Model Degradation

A wrong spread or invented guaranty number is an operational incident.

  • Evaluator rejects ungrounded claims (no artefact id).
  • guarantyNumber is written only from E-Tran — never inferred.
  • Drift monitors on extraction confidence and scorecard override rate.
  • Rollback to last signed prompt/model pair; human queue absorbs volume.

12 Model Decommissioning

Retire a model only after a dual-run against the successor, MRM sign-off, and archive of prompts, eval sets, and audit hashes. Indexes tagged to the old embedder are rebuilt, not mixed.

13 Right to be Forgotten

Erasure walks Customer → ApplicantAccount → Address → pulls → embeddings tagged with that partyId. Sealed process rows remain as required audit if the bank must retain the file.

Raw SSN is never in prompts, lake feeds, or indexes — only taxIdToken and last four. Lake copies are in the cascade.

14 Cross-Border Data Transfer

Credit files and CIP stay in the named residency region. Offshore L2 support sees tokenized workbenches only, under existing vendor contracts. No bulk extract of origination files to a non-approved region.

15 Green AI / Sustainability

Token and tool cost is a control. Prefer retrieval over re-generation; cache bureau artefacts; do not re-pull FICO on agent retry.

  • Report kWh / process monthly (model + OCR + vector).
  • Cap max worker fan-out per process.
  • Archive cold embeddings rather than re-embed nightly.

17 Vendor Lock-in Mitigation

Prompts, eval sets, and the canonical schema (Process, Customer, Business, Loan) are bank IP. Model and OCR vendors are interchangeable behind adapters. Lake events stay resource-oriented HTTP, not a vendor RPC.

23 ROI & Value Realization

Value is shorter cycle time and cleaner files, with the same (or tighter) credit and BSA posture. Do not count hours the underwriter still owes as “automated.”

Track benefits against a pre-swarm baseline per product (7(a) vs Express vs 504) after a monitored parallel-run, not at go-live day one.
